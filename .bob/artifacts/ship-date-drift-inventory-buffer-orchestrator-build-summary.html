4-Step Orchestration Workflow

1
Inbound Event Ingestion & Model Armor Screening

Supplier email / carrier webhook received → Model Armor scans for prompt injection, PII, tool poisoning → sanitized payload passed to runtime.

2
Context Retrieval & ERP Impact Analysis

Memory Bank retrieves vendor SLA (Clause 14B), reliability score, and delivery history. ERP/SAP queried via OIDC token → BOM reverse-lookup → impacted Sales Orders identified → revenue at risk calculated.

3
Sub-Agent Coordination via A2A Gateway

Gateway enforces policy (max auto-freight $50k, max delay 10 days). Warehouse Sub-Agent creates WTO if buffer below reorder point. Freight Sub-Agent recommends air/ocean/ground and creates request.

4
Remediation Staging & Human Sign-Off

ERP PO updated, Sales Orders revised, draft vendor credit claim generated. Full reasoning chain written to Audit Ledger. 1-click Approve / Reject card pushed to dashboard via WebSocket.
